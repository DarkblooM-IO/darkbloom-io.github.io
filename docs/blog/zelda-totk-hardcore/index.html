Hardcore challenge rules for Zelda TotK

Note

These are subject to change

Goal(s)

  • Complete all main quests

Rules

  • Can't use Amiibos
  • Must use pro HUD
  • Can't use the paraglider
  • Can't fast travel
  • Permadeath
  • Can't use Fuse (including arrows)
  • Can't eat during battle
  • No special effect food/potions

The following glitches are banned:

  • Any form of duplication (items, weapons or Zonai devices)
  • Zuggling
  • Fuse entanglement (including variants involving Mineru)
  • Auto-Build Cancel
  • Arrow smuggling
